# Profile backgrounds in the admin

1. Open **Shop** in the admin and choose **Add New Item** (or edit an existing background).
2. Select **Profile Background**, enter its name and purchase price, and upload the artwork.
3. Check the full-profile preview. For animated artwork, choose **Play background** to preview motion.
4. Save the item. Readers can purchase it and equip it from the shop or their profile inventory.

An equipped background covers the entire own and public profile, behind the avatar, activity, collection and other sections. The image fills the viewport and stays visible while scrolling. It is centered and cropped to fit each screen, so keep important details near the center and check both desktop and mobile.

PNG, JPEG, SVG, WebP and GIF uploads are supported, up to **20 MiB**. Animated **GIF/WebP** backgrounds retain their animation and are stored as WebP, with a separate still preview. Animated uploads may contain at most **120 frames**, a **30-second cycle**, **4 million pixels per frame**, and **40 million decoded pixels in total**. Animated artwork is resized to at most **1920 px** on its longest side.

Readers have **Pause background / Play background** controls. Their operating system's reduced-motion preference starts the background on its still preview. Admin previews also start paused.

Previously uploaded animations were saved as still images. To restore animation, edit the background and upload its **original GIF/WebP** again. Existing static backgrounds automatically use the full-profile layout.
