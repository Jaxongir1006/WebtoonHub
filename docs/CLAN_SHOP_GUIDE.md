# Clan decorations

Clan frames and backgrounds come from the existing shop catalog. A personal
purchase belongs to the reader; a clan purchase belongs to that clan. Buying an
item for yourself does not automatically give it to your clan.

## Buy and equip

1. Open your clan and select **Clan Shop**.
2. A leader or co-leader can choose **Purchase** on a frame or background.
3. Confirm the displayed price. The acting leader/co-leader pays from their own
   lightning balance, and the decoration is added to the clan inventory.
4. Choose **Equip** in the clan inventory. One frame and one background can be
   equipped at a time. **Unequip** removes the decoration without losing ownership.

Other clan members can browse the shop and inventory. Purchases stay with the
clan when the buyer leaves or leadership changes. Character cards remain
personal collectibles obtained through Character Card Gacha.

The equipped background fills the entire clan page behind its header, chat,
members and shop. It stays visible while scrolling. Animated GIF/WebP backgrounds
use the same pause/play and reduced-motion behavior as profile backgrounds.

## Admin artwork

Create frames and backgrounds through **Admin → Shop** with a purchase price.
These same items are offered in personal and clan shops. Frame artwork needs a
transparent center; see [the frame guide](ADMIN_AVATAR_FRAMES_GUIDE.md). For
background upload formats and limits, see [the background guide](ADMIN_PROFILE_BACKGROUNDS_GUIDE.md).

Clan settings still allow a custom logo, description and recruitment status.
Frame/background uploads and arbitrary decoration URLs are no longer accepted.
Older direct decoration URLs are retained in the database for recovery, but are
not displayed as purchased clan items. No personal inventory or past purchases
are transferred automatically.
